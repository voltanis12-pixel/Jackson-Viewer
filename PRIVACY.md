# Jackson Viewer Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

## Overview

Jackson Viewer is an independently maintained third-party viewer for Second Life.

This privacy policy describes data handling specific to Jackson Viewer. It does not replace the privacy policies or terms of Linden Lab, Second Life, voice providers, websites, media services, or other third-party services that may be accessed through the viewer.

## Jackson Viewer Data Collection

Jackson Viewer RC2 does not include Jackson-operated analytics or telemetry services.

Jackson Viewer RC2 is built with BugSplat disabled and with release and developer crash reporting disabled. Jackson Viewer does not automatically send crash reports to the Jackson Viewer project.

The Jackson Viewer modifications included in RC2 do not add a Jackson-specific analytics, telemetry, webhook, crash-reporting, or tracking endpoint.

## Second Life Services

Jackson Viewer must communicate with Second Life and Linden Lab services in order to provide normal viewer functionality such as authentication, regions, inventory, assets, messaging, profiles, and other Second Life features.

Data transmitted to or processed by Linden Lab is governed by Linden Lab and Second Life policies, not by the Jackson Viewer project.

## Third-Party Content and Services

Some viewer features may access third-party services when you choose to use them. Examples can include embedded web content, streaming media, voice services, and external links.

Those services may receive network information or other data according to their own privacy policies. Jackson Viewer does not control those third-party services.

## Local Data

Like the upstream Second Life Viewer, Jackson Viewer may store viewer settings, caches, logs, and other application data locally on your computer in order to operate normally.

Users can uninstall Jackson Viewer through Windows. Locally stored application data may require separate removal depending on Windows and viewer settings.

## Crash Reporting

For Jackson Viewer 26.4.0.262540502 RC2:

- BugSplat: Disabled
- Release crash reporting: Disabled
- Developer crash reporting: Disabled
- Jackson-operated crash-reporting endpoint: None

## Support and Privacy Questions

Questions about Jackson Viewer-specific privacy behavior may be submitted through the Jackson Viewer GitHub Issues page.

For privacy questions involving Second Life accounts, Linden Lab services, or data controlled by Linden Lab, contact Linden Lab through its official support and privacy channels.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if future Jackson Viewer releases add services or features that change how data is collected, stored, used, or shared.
